Quinoa White Bean Salad with Creamy Lemon Dressing

5 from 6 votes

A protein-rich quinoa and white bean salad with a creamy lemon dressing. 20 minutes, simple ingredients, perfect for meal prep.

Modified: September 24, 2026 by Nutritionist · This post may contain affiliate links · 14 Comments

↓ Jump to Recipe

This quinoa white bean salad is packed with protein and fibre, made from simple ingredients and keeps in the fridge for up to four days. Step-by-step instructions and a printable recipe card are below.

Why this recipe works

  • Protein without meat: quinoa and white beans together provide a complete set of amino acids.
  • Fibre for your gut: over 12 g per serving.
  • One bowl: everything comes together in one bowl in 20 minutes.
  • Budget-friendly: no exotic ingredients.

Ingredients

The full list with quantities is in the recipe card below. The essentials: cooked quinoa, canned white beans, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, parsley, lemon, tahini and a clove of garlic.

How to make it

  1. Rinse the quinoa and simmer in salted water for 15 minutes, then let it cool.
  2. Whisk the dressing: tahini, lemon juice, garlic, a pinch of salt and 2–3 tablespoons of water until creamy.
  3. Combine the quinoa, beans, chopped vegetables and herbs, then pour over the dressing.
  4. Let it rest for 10 minutes or refrigerate for later.

Quinoa White Bean Salad with Creamy Lemon Dressing

A protein-rich meal-prep salad with a creamy lemon dressing.

Prep 10 minCook 15 minTotal 25 minServings 4
Print Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa (dry)
  • 1 can (400 g) white beans
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes
  • ½ bunch parsley
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 clove garlic
  • salt, pepper

Cook

  1. Cook the quinoa and let it cool.
  2. Whisk the dressing until creamy.
  3. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and pour over the dressing.
  4. Let it rest for 10 minutes. Keeps for up to 4 days.

Nutrition (per serving)

Calories 320 · Protein 14 g · Fibre 12 g · Fat 9 g · Carbs 46 g
